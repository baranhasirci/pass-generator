    How it works

    No passwords are stored. Each password is computed from your master secret, the site, and the options. The same inputs always give the same password, on any device.

    Slow on purpose, once. Your master secret is stretched with PBKDF2-SHA256 (1,000,000 iterations), so every guess costs an attacker the same work. That happens once per session. Site passwords are then derived with HMAC-SHA256, which is instant.

    Fingerprint. The three icons next to your master secret come from it. Learn yours: if they ever look different, you mistyped.

    Version. Increase it to get a fresh password for one site without changing anything else.

    100% local. The app makes no network requests. Its Content Security Policy forbids them. It works offline once loaded.
